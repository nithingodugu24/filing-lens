# FilingLens: report to an actionable brief

The product serves people who need to understand an Indian company's financial report without reading hundreds of pages. The first useful result is a clear number or explanation with its exact reporting period, accounting basis, units and source page. A feature count or a synthetic benchmark is not proof of readiness for real users.

The core journey is: open a real report or upload your own → receive a source-linked brief and automatically checked financial rows → ask a question → inspect the evidence → compare periods or export your work.

## Delivery gates

1. **Reliable financial answers.** The reported failure question, “what is the total income in crores this year”, must resolve total income rather than income-tax expense or lease liabilities. Explicit table headers establish the year and standalone/consolidated columns. Decimal accounting identities and literal cell references gate automatic publication. No missing digits are invented. Automated checks and human review have distinct labels. The latest year means the latest period in the selected report, which is shown.
2. **A useful report home.** Opening a report shows a brief, revenue/total-income/profit figures where extractable, prior-year comparisons from the same report, source buttons and clear processing states. A single annual report can contain two years of useful financial data. Empty widgets do not occupy the main workflow. The first screen explains the outcome and offers real prepared reports.
3. **A genuine example library.** Official Infosys and Wipro annual reports are downloaded from issuer URLs with hashes/provenance. PDF files remain out of Git. Each prepared example needs completed indexing, a sourced brief and checked row mappings. The old synthetic fixtures remain test fixtures, not the primary product experience. Example numbers in feedback are not treated as data.
4. **Useful exploration, not a graph hairball.** The main navigation focuses on the report brief, questions and reports. Source-linked key people/organisations are a secondary report tool with bounded results and type/count explanations. Sentence proximity is never relabelled as a verified executive/subsidiary relationship.
5. **A launch boundary that is enforced.** Local development can remain convenient; production configuration must require authentication, deliberate administrator setup, explicit origins and safe secrets. Ownership applies to source files and derived output. Users need durable report/history controls and visible failures. Independent financial accuracy, recovery, abuse/load testing and a deployment/operating plan remain launch gates, not claims inferred from passing unit tests.

## Evidence of improvement

Keep the exact reported failure as an acceptance case. Also cover fiscal-year selection, standalone versus consolidated, lakh/million/crore conversion, revenue versus total income, ambiguous short questions, conflicting/restated numbers, source access controls and failure recovery. Verify prepared-report metrics against the actual PDF pages. Measure time to first useful result and successful task completion on real reports; do not represent small synthetic recall results as market validation.

The user has explicitly rejected college-project positioning. New product screens and guidance should describe practical outcomes. Historical development/evaluation records remain records of what was tested, not a marketing claim of commercial readiness.
